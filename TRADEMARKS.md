or Abstain on them. Its code is open source under the Apache License 2.0. Its name and logos are not. This page says
how you may use them. "We" means the project's maintainers.

## What this page covers

- **The name "Billboard"**, as the name of this app, service and project. We do not claim the ordinary word
  "billboard". Other organizations use the name for unrelated products, and this project is not connected with them.
- **The logos:**
  - the app icon, `Billboard/Billboard/AppIcon.icon/` in billboard-ios;
  - the website icons, `website/assets/favicon.svg`, `website/favicon.ico` and `website/apple-touch-icon.png` in
    billboard-backend;
  - the share image, `website/assets/share-preview.png` in billboard-backend, shown when a link to the site or to a
    bill is shared.

On this page, "the marks" means all of the above.

## You may

- Refer to Billboard truthfully, by name: in news, reviews, research, teaching, talks and posts.
- Describe your own work truthfully, for example "based on Billboard", "a fork of Billboard" or "uses code from
  billboard-backend".
- Link to the official app, website or repositories. You may show the unchanged icon next to that link.
- Build the app from source and run it yourself, to try it, test it or work on the project, with the name and icon as
  they are. Do not give that build to other people.
- Deploy the backend in your own account to try or test it, as long as you do not open it to the public under the
  Billboard name.
- Keep the repository name in a fork.
- Leave the name where it appears in code: identifiers, file and package names, comments and history. A fork does not
  have to rename them.

- Suggest that we made, approve, sponsor or support your app, service, fork or organization, or that it is the

- Use the marks to suggest that the project supports or opposes a candidate, party, bill or cause.
- Present votes, results or reports as Billboard's unless they come unchanged from the official service.
- Change the logos, or combine them with your own name or logo.
- Use the marks in a product name, domain name or social media handle, or on merchandise, without our written

## If you ship a fork

Give it its own name and its own icon. Use your own bundle ID and URL scheme. Change the places where users see the
Billboard name: the display name, the App Store listing, the About screen and its disclaimer, notifications, and the
website and share links. You may say "Based on Billboard" in your description or About screen.

CONTRIBUTING.md also asks you to run your own backend for a build you ship.
